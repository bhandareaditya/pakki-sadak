Start my case

  1. Step 1

    File on Sahaaya

    Pakki Sadak writes the complaint with your exact spot, photo and dates. Send it on Sahaaya’s WhatsApp in one tap and note the ticket number.

  2. Step 2

    Call your ward engineer

    Type your street and the ward engineer’s number appears, with a call script and a WhatsApp message that already quotes your ticket.

  3. Step 3

    Tell whoever dug it

    If BWSSB, BESCOM or a cable company cut the road, they owe the repair. One tap sends them your ticket and photos.

  4. Step 4

    File the RTI

    Still no repair date? Your RTI is already written: who cut the road, the restoration deadline, the officer responsible. ₹10, reply due in 30 days.

  5. Step 5

    Go public

    Put your case on the public board with a counter that keeps climbing. Neighbours back it from one link, so it reads as a street’s problem.

  6. Fixed

    Relaid, with proof

    Add the after photo. Pakki Sadak puts it next to the before, counts the days and adds your street to the wall of fixed roads.
